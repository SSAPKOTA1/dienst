# User manual (English)

For **employees**, the **tablet** and **managers** (planners). The application is available in German and English; switch the language at the top right.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the address your hotel gave you.
2. **First time:** you receive an invitation e-mail (or an activation code from your manager if you have no e-mail). Open _Accept invitation_, enter your username (when using a code) and a password of **at least 10 characters**.
3. **Afterwards:** username or e-mail and password. With several roles or hotels you pick a role next. Change it later from your name menu at the top right (_Switch role_).
4. **Forgot password:** use _Forgot password_ on the sign-in page; the link arrives by e-mail.
5. Five wrong passwords in a row lock the account briefly; your manager can help.

Managers and administrators also sign in with a code from an authenticator app or with a security key/passkey (section 7).

## 2. Employee portal (phone)

Sections: **Start, Schedule, Times, Vacation, Team, Account**. You can add the page to your home screen as an app.

- **Start:** your next shift, punch status, open notes and announcements. Announcements that need confirmation must be confirmed.
- **Schedule:** your published shifts (drafts are not visible). Subscribe to the calendar from _Account_. To **swap**, pick a shift and offer it or ask a colleague; the manager must approve. You can view and apply for **open shifts**.
- **Times:** your attendance, breaks and status (_Recorded_, _Approved_). If a punch is missing, file a **time correction** with a reason (forgotten in/out, wrong time, missing day). The manager decides.
- **Vacation:** entitlement, used and remaining days. Submit a **vacation request** with from/to; the preview shows working days and notices (blackout periods, not enough days) before you send. Half days are possible. Shift wishes go under _Account → My wishes_; questions to management under _Team_.
- **Team:** colleagues, announcements, posts, questions to management.
- **Account:** change password, language, calendar link, qualifications and documents (e.g. hygiene instruction), availability.

You do **not** report sickness yourself: call your manager, who records it on the schedule.

## 3. Punching at the tablet

1. **Tap your name** (or _Search name_ if you are not scheduled today), then enter your **6-digit PIN**. Alternatively hold your **badge** to the reader, then enter the PIN.
2. Choose **Clock in**, **Break** or **Clock out**. The tablet shows what was recorded; _Not you?_ cancels.
3. **Breaks:** the legal break (e.g. 30 minutes after more than 6 hours) is proposed and confirmed with you. If the real break was shorter you must give a reason. Starting and ending a break is possible where your hotel uses it.
4. **Unplanned work** can be punched; it is flagged and your manager reviews the time.
5. **No connection:** the tablet stores punches and sends them later; you see "Saved offline".
6. Five wrong PIN entries lock your PIN for 15 minutes. Ask your manager for a new PIN; it is shown to you **once**. Never share it.

If you forget to clock out the system closes the entry after a grace period and flags it for review. File a time correction.

## 4. Managers: the schedule

Menu **Planning → Schedule** (Dienstplan).

- **View:** week with employees in rows or grouped by shift; filter hotels and departments.
- **Create a shift:** plus menu in the cell, or **drag and drop**. Keyboard: focus a cell, Enter, arrow keys; every action works without a mouse.
- **Rule checks:** the server checks each change against the working-time act, youth protection and hotel rules. **Blocked** (red) cannot be saved except with the **emergency override** and a reason (logged). **Warnings** (yellow) come with an explanation. The server decides what is allowed, never the screen.
- **Draft and publish:** changes start as drafts. **Publish** makes them visible and notifies staff; the change panel shows what changed and can revert.
- **Find a substitute:** suggests suitable people (rest period, hours, qualification).
- **Absences** (vacation, sickness, other) are entered on the schedule. Copy or clear a week to save time.
- **More:** _Month overview_, _Vacation_ (planner with blackout periods, wishes, notices), _Open shifts_, _Staffing_ (demand suggestions from occupancy).

## 5. Managers: Today

- **Live:** who is clocked in, who is missing, entries to review. Close open entries with time, break and reason as a correction; not for yourself.
- **Requests:** inbox for times, corrections, vacation requests and swaps. **Approve**/**Reject** with a note; unusual entries can be adjusted. You do not decide your own requests. The badge on the tab shows open requests.
- **Announcements:** messages to teams with optional read confirmation.
- **Compliance:** replacement rest (a shortened rest must be compensated within four weeks), Sunday and night work. Red means the deadline passed.

## 6. Managers: Team

- **Staff:** search, details, time account, vacation, contract data, **reset PIN**, **unlock**, assign badge, deactivate. Create people with _Create employee_ or the **Excel import** (dry run, error list, one-time credentials sheet). Every person automatically gets an account.
- **Analytics:** figures and trends for planning. Hours only; no wages, no ranking of individuals.
- **Month close** (administration): when all times are approved the month is closed; changes are locked until it is reopened with a reason.

## 7. Securing your account

Under _Security_ in the name menu (managers/administrators) add or remove **security keys or passkeys**. A key signs you in without a password. Always keep a second way to sign in.

## 8. FAQ

- **I cannot see my shift.** It may still be a draft; ask your manager.
- **My PIN does not work.** Check you are using your own name; after five mistakes it is locked for 15 minutes.
- **Why is my paid time shorter?** Legal breaks are deducted; the manager can adjust paid time with a note. Look for the note under _Times_.
- **Who sees my data?** Only authorised people; health details are never shown, only "absent". For access and deletion see your hotel's privacy notice.
