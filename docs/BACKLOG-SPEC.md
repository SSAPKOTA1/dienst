# Backlog build (SPEC section 10), built after v1 at the owner's request

Scope: everything in SPEC §10 except **biometrics, employee sick reporting and GPS** (stay excluded). **Wage/hourly-rate fields stay excluded** (owner decision): the payroll export carries hours and hour categories only. SSO, a public API and an installable PWA (instead of native apps) are built as far as feasible without external systems. SPEC.md sections 1-9 remain valid; where this file adds endpoints or rules it extends them. Order of authority is unchanged: SPEC.md, DESIGN.md, db schema, tests, then this file for backlog features.

Milestones: M10 Leave, M11 Hours, M12 Collaboration, M13 Platform. Migrations 005-008, one per milestone.

## M10 Leave
- **Blackout periods** (`absence_blackout`): `GET/POST /blackouts`, `PUT/DELETE /blackouts/:id` (SA, AD, MG of the hotel), `GET /me/blackouts`. `maxConcurrentAbsent = null` means no vacation at all; a number caps simultaneous absences of the employee's department (approved absences of other employees). `department.max_concurrent_absent` is the default cap.
- **Limits are enforced as rules** (`packages/rules/src/leave.ts`, `absenceLimitIssues`): employee requests are blocked (422 `RULE_BLOCKED`, codes `BLACKOUT`, `MAX_CONCURRENT`), planners need a reason (`REASON_REQUIRED`, audited override), the inbox shows `conflicts[]` per request.
- **Half-day vacation**: `halfDay: morning|afternoon` on `POST /schedule/absence` and `POST /me/time-off-requests` (single day, annual leave): counts 0.5, does not cancel the day's entries and does not block planning the other half.
- **Vacation planner** `GET /vacation/overview?year&hotelIds`: per employee entitlement, carryover, taken, planned, requested, remaining, month sums and entries (table and year overview in `/vacation`).
- **Wishes**: employees `POST/GET/DELETE /me/shift-wishes`, `/me/leave-wishes` (priority 1-3); planners `GET /wishes`, `PUT /wishes/shift|leave/:id {decision: grant|decline, note?}`. A pending or granted wish that an entry contradicts yields the warning `WISH_CONFLICT` ("Widerspricht Wunsch"). A wish is never a promise and creates no entry.
- **Vacation notices and carryover**: `POST /vacation/carryover {year}`, `POST /vacation/notices/send {year, kind}`, `GET /vacation/notices`, `GET /me/vacation-notices`, `PUT /me/vacation-notices/:id/ack`. Daily job: carryover 1-7 Jan, expiry of unused carryover on 1 April only for employees who received a notice, notices 1 Oct (initial), 15 Nov (reminder), 1 Dec (final).
- **Month overview** `/month`: read-only range view from `/schedule/grid?range=month`.

## M11 Hours
- **Time-account ledger**: `GET /employees/:id/time-account/ledger` (AD, SA, MG of the home hotel) and `GET /me/time-account/ledger`: month lines `opening_balance`, `worked`, `absence_credit`, `target` (derived, their sum equals the v1 balance) plus stored manual lines `correction` and `payout`. `POST /employees/:id/time-account/entries {date, type: correction|payout, hours, note}` (AD, SA, audited; a payout is stored negative). `computeTimeAccount` is now `buildLedger(...).balanceHours`.
- **Absence types** from SPEC §10 are available in the planner's "Mehr" menu: `comp_time` (salary workers only; reason needed when the account would go negative, `COMP_TIME_EXCEEDS`), `special_leave`, `child_sick`, `training`, `parental_leave`, `maternity_leave`, `rest_day`.
- **Hour categories** (`hour_category`): defaults night 23-6, Sunday, public holiday, 24/31 December from 14:00 live in `packages/rules` (`DEFAULT_CATEGORIES`); a company row with the same code overrides or (`active=false`) disables one; `GET/POST /hour-categories`, `PUT/DELETE /hour-categories/:id` (AD, SA). Minutes are computed from the paid window split at hotel-local midnights (DST safe) and holiday calendar; recorded break segments are deducted.
- **Payroll export** `GET /payroll/export?month&hotelIds&format=json|csv|xlsx&includeOpen` (AD, SA): per personnel number worked hours, hours per category, absence days per type, time account. CSV/Excel only for a closed month unless `includeOpen=true`. Never wages.
- **Rule profiles** (`rule_profile`, company and hotel override): `GET/PUT/DELETE /settings/rules`. Eight limits (daily max/warn, rest block/warn, minor rest and daily max, free Sundays, night-worker nights); the API accepts only values stricter than the statutory baseline (`validateLimits`). The planning checks read them (`CheckInput.limits`).
- **Sunday and night work**: warnings `SUNDAY_LIMIT` (fewer than the required free Sundays in the calendar year) and `NIGHT_WORKER` (night entries = at least 2 h between 23:00 and 06:00, counted over 12 months). `GET /compliance/sundays-nights`.
- **Rest-period compensation** (ArbZG § 5 Abs. 2): `GET /compliance/rest-compensation` lists every rest of at least 10 h and under 11 h with the due date (4 weeks) and whether a rest of at least 12 h compensated it. **Replacement rest days** `GET /compliance/replacement-rest`: Sunday work needs an approved `rest_day` within 14 days, holiday work within 56 days.
- **Feature toggles** (`company_feature`): `GET/PUT /settings/features`, `GET /me/features`; employee endpoints of a switched-off feature answer 403 `FEATURE_DISABLED`. Features: wishes, swaps, open_shifts, availability, announcements, feed, messages, documents, calendar_feed, team_calendar.
- **Analytics** `GET /analytics/summary?from&to&hotelIds`: planned vs actual hours, time-account totals and absence rate per department, approvals aging, rule overrides, correction rate, open slots. No per-person figures; absence rates only from 5 employees per department.
- UI: `/compliance`, `/analytics`, editable Regeln tab (limits, categories, features), payroll export in the admin overview, ledger in staff detail and portal.

## M12 Collaboration
- **Shift swaps and giveaways** (`shift_swap_request`, feature `swaps`): `POST /me/swap-requests {scheduleId, counterpartEmployeeId?, counterpartScheduleId?, reason?}` (published shifts that start in more than 12 h; a counterpart shift makes it a 1:1 swap; no counterpart = offered to every eligible colleague of the hotel), `GET /me/swap-requests`, `PUT /me/swap-requests/:id/accept|decline`, `DELETE /me/swap-requests/:id`; lookups `GET /me/colleagues`, `GET /me/colleagues/:id/shifts`. The receiving person is checked with the full planning rules (blocking rules refuse the request; reasons are for the planner). Accepted requests go to `GET /approvals/swaps` and `PUT /approvals/swaps/:id {decision, note?, overrideReason?}` which runs the real planning operation (update or swap, audited, notifications). `company.swap_approval = 'auto_if_valid'` approves at once when the rule check has no violation beyond warnings. Requests expire after 48 h or 12 h before the shift (job).
- **Open shifts** (`open_shift`, `open_shift_claim`, feature `open_shifts`): planners `POST /open-shifts`, `GET /open-shifts`, `DELETE /open-shifts/:id`, decide `PUT /open-shifts/claims/:id {decision, overrideReason?}` (approve creates the entry as a draft, rejects the other applications); employees `GET /me/open-shifts` (only slots they may work), `POST/DELETE /me/open-shifts/:id/claim`.
- **Availability** (feature `availability`): `GET/POST/DELETE /me/availability`, planners `GET /availability`. Planning into a "cannot work" window needs a reason (`UNAVAILABLE`, rule in `packages/rules`); preferred windows are data for the UI.
- **Qualifications**: `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /qualifications`, `GET/PUT /employees/:id/qualifications`, `GET /me/qualifications`, `shift.requiredQualificationId`. A missing or expired qualification warns (`QUALIFICATION_MISSING`). Expiry reminders to the admins 30 and 7 days before (daily job).
- **Documents** (feature `documents`, no health data): `POST /employees/:id/documents` (multipart, PDF/PNG/JPEG by content sniffing, 10 MB, types contract, hygiene_instruction, work_permit, training_certificate, payslip, other), `GET /employees/:id/documents`, `GET /documents/:id/download`, `DELETE /documents/:id`, `GET /documents/expiring`, employee `GET /me/documents` and download (only `visibleToEmployee`). Stored AES-256-GCM encrypted; every download is audit-logged.
- **Offboarding**: `POST /employees/:id/terminate {lastDay, reason?}` (AD, SA) sets the contract end, removes drafts and cancels published entries after the last day, returns the exit statement (`GET /employees/:id/exit-statement`: remaining vacation to pay out under BUrlG § 7 Abs. 4, time account, open items). A daily job deactivates PIN and login after the last day; contract-end alerts 30 and 14 days before.
- **Announcements** (feature `announcements`): `POST/GET/DELETE /announcements` (company-wide by AD/SA, hotel-wide also by MG), `GET /me/announcements`, `PUT /me/announcements/:id/ack`; optional read confirmation with the list of people who have not confirmed.
- **Questions to management** (feature `messages`): `POST/GET /me/questions`, `GET /questions`, `PUT /questions/:id/answer`; open questions count in `/approvals/count` (`questions`).
- **Feed** (feature `feed`): hotel board with posts, comments, likes (`GET /feed`, `POST /feed/posts`, `DELETE /feed/posts/:id`, `POST /feed/posts/:id/comments`, `DELETE /feed/comments/:id`, `PUT /feed/posts/:id/like`). Display names are "First L."; authors and planners can delete (moderation is audit-logged). Plain text only.
- **Calendar subscription** (feature `calendar_feed`): `POST /me/calendar-feed` returns a secret URL once (only the hash is stored; creating a new one revokes the old), `DELETE /me/calendar-feed`, public `GET /feeds/:token.ics` with published shifts and generic "Abwesend" days.
- **Team absences** (feature `team_calendar`, company setting `team_absence_visibility = names_only`, `PUT /settings/team-visibility`): `GET /me/team-absences` shows colleagues of the department as away without type or reason; sickness, child sick, parental and maternity leave are never shown.
- UI: portal tab Team (announcements, swaps, open shifts, team absences, questions, feed), swap button in the schedule, account extras (availability, qualifications, documents, calendar link); planner pages Offene Schichten, Mitteilungen (with feed), and in Anträge swaps, applications and questions; staff detail with qualifications, documents and offboarding; Regeln tab with qualifications and the team-visibility switch.
